About the bookstore

Build a recognisable local brand and make it easier for people to discover, visit, and buy from the store.

Project objective

Use digital marketing to improve the bookstore’s local visibility, bring more customers into the store, increase purchase frequency, and encourage repeat sales.

03 · Search visibility

Help nearby readers find the store

Use location-specific wording and accurate business information to improve discovery on search and maps.

Local SEO actions

  • Claim and update the Google Business Profile.
  • Add store hours, phone number, directions, and fresh photos.
  • List popular categories and services accurately.
  • Invite customers to leave honest reviews.
  • Keep the name, address, and phone number consistent online.

07 · Recommendations & measurement

Turn ideas into growth

Prioritise low-cost improvements, measure what changes, and invest more in tactics that produce profitable sales.

01 · Loyalty card

Reward repeat purchases with a simple stamp or points system; set rules that preserve margins.

02 · Book bundles

Create relevant bundles for exams, gifting, children’s reading, or themed book clubs.

03 · Community events

Run a monthly reading circle, book exchange, author discussion, or children’s storytelling session.

04 · Partnerships

Connect with schools, colleges, tutors, libraries, and local clubs for legitimate group offers.

05 · Simple ordering

Let customers ask about availability or reserve a book through WhatsApp Business or a simple order form.

06 · Track the numbers

Record weekly sales, transactions, average order value, repeat customers, campaign costs, and coupon redemptions.
